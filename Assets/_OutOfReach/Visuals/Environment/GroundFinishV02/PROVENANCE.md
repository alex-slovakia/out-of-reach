Official API metadata and source/derived SHA-256 receipts are in docs/art-direction/ground-finish-20261115/. Retrieved15 November2026. The license covers commercial video-game use and redistribution of raw maps. The two2K photographic materials are converted to8-bit Unity albedo/OpenGL normal and packed AO/height/roughness maps. No generated bitmap substitute is used. Ground geometry and shader blending are original project work; the pre-existing LandTerrainV01 macro geography is retained.

TERRAIN-V03 (15 November 2026): `GroundFinishV02.shader` is now "OutOfReach/Launch Ground V03" (same asset GUID). It samples only Ground109 (colour luminance, normal and mask) as fading micro detail; Ground110 and the LandTerrainV01 macro map are no longer sampled (their bindings stay on the material for compatibility). The grass palette, broad transitions, meadow mottling, scrub/rock zones and haze are original shader code. The same material also shades the generated far land (`Visuals/LaunchLandscapeV02/Generated/Far land V03 sector *.asset`). No new downloads.

# LaunchComplexV01 provenance

- **Geometry**: original, generated by `Source~/build_launch_complex_v01.py` (Blender 3.6.1, Python). No imported
  meshes, no scans, no third-party kits. The launch vehicle is a fictional design; it carries no agency logo, flag or
  real programme marking. Lettering on the models is `AURORA`, `7`, `LOX` and `LCH4` only.
- **Textures**: original, generated by `Source~/lc1_tex.py` (numpy value noise with fixed seeds, byte-reproducible).
  No photographs, no downloaded texture sets.
- **Lettering glyphs**: the outlines of the letters come from the font bundled with Blender 3.6 (`Bfont`, a
  Bitstream Vera Sans derivative shipped under the Bitstream Vera Fonts licence, which permits use, copying and
  distribution of the font and of works made with it). Only the resulting meshes are in the FBX; no font file is
  shipped in this folder.
- **Reference**: general public knowledge of how launch pads are laid out (flame trench, deluge, lightning masts,
  propellant farm). No drawing, model or texture of a real vehicle or pad was copied.
- Licence of this folder: same as the repository.

## Known problems
- Habitat: the fittings and connectors on the grow cassette's side faces lie 2.5 mm inside the `COL_Habitat_GrowCassette` box. That box is larger than the cassette mesh. There is no mesh overlap.
- Habitat: the board photo reuses `photo_hills` from `HB_Labels`, which also appears inside the berth.
- Material slots are `null` in the isolated Unity project because the A7 materials are not there. Remapping in the game project has not been tested.
- The FBX `.meta` GUIDs are md5 values of the asset path (`make_metas.py`). They are stable but were not generated by Unity. The isolated Unity import read them and did not rewrite the FBX `.meta`.

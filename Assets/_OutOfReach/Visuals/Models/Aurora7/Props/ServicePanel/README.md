# ServicePanel — EPS-2 power distribution panel

Hand-off package from the panel author (Claude chat "Blender fork") to the repository model writer (CODEX-006).
It was authored outside the repository in Blender 5.3 from a script and checked in a private Unity sandbox.
Move this folder as a whole, **with every `.meta` file**, to its place under `Assets/_OutOfReach/Visuals/Models/Aurora7/`.

Wall-mounted electrical service panel for the **PowerRepair** location: graphite frame, light painted enclosure,
three status lamps, eight guarded circuit breakers, a hinged service cover with a real cavity behind it
(bus bars, fuses, terminal block) and four circular connectors. It contains no gameplay state, scripts, camera
or audio listener.

## Files

- `ServicePanel.prefab` — use this. A variant of the model with the materials and the collision proxy.
- `ServicePanel.fbx` — three triangulated meshes with exported normals. Its `.meta` remaps the five materials to `Materials/`.
- `Materials/`, `Textures/` — as listed above. The textures are clamped atlases; MetallicSmoothness and Occlusion are linear.
- `Previews/` — five genuine Cycles renders of this mesh with these textures (`hero`, `front`, `detail`, `open`,
  `wire`) and two captures made in Unity by the import check (`ServicePanel_unity_urp.png`,
  `ServicePanel_unity_urp_open.png`). The Unity captures use two plain directional lights and no tone mapping;
  they show that the import works, not final lighting.
- `Source/` — `build_service_panel.py` regenerates the model, label atlas, baked textures, renders, `.blend` and FBX:
  `blender -b --factory-startup --python build_service_panel.py -- --stage full --out <folder outside Assets>`.
  `verify_fbx.py` re-imports the FBX and reports what a consumer receives. `ServicePanel.blend.bytes` is the saved
  `.blend`; the extra extension stops Unity from importing it through Blender. Rename it to open it.

## What must stay together

The prefab points at the FBX and the materials by GUID, the materials point at the textures by GUID, and the
FBX `.meta` holds the material remap and import settings. Keep the `.meta` of every **file** unchanged. Folder
`.meta` files may be regenerated, and the folder may be renamed or moved freely.

## Integration notes

- **Placement.** Wall-mounted. To centre it on PowerRepair (anchor height 1.15 m), put the pivot 0.55 m above the
  floor with the back plane on the wall and local +Z pointing into the room. No anchor or layout change is requested.
- **Cover.** `ServiceCover` has its pivot on the hinge axis, at local (0.2575, 0.24, 0.046) under the root; the
  hinge is on the viewer's left. Rotate it about its local Y: 0° is closed, **+100°** is fully open and swings
  out towards +Z. It is not animated.
- **Lamps.** `StatusIndicators` has one submesh per lamp, in the order cyan (BUS OK), amber (CAUTION), red (FAULT).
  Drive a lamp through `_EmissionColor` on its material instance. Delivered values stay within 0–1 so the hue
  survives without tone mapping: cyan and amber lit, red dim. Lamps are presentation only; they must follow
  `SystemConditionChanged` and decide nothing.
- **Breaker CB6 (PUMP B)** is modelled tripped (lever down) as a small story cue. All levers are part of `Body`.
- **Occlusion** was baked per part without its neighbours, so opening the cover reveals no baked shadow.
- **First-frame note.** In a batch run the first URP frame drew the emissive lamps in magenta, while later
  frames of the same run were correct: the emission shader variant was still compiling. The import check now
  compiles synchronously and renders warm-up frames before reading a capture back; do the same before judging a
  first screenshot.

## Limits

- One LOD, no lightmap UVs, no normal map. Wear is deliberately light: roughness variation, grime in recesses
  and sparse edge chips.
- Base textures are about 1 mm per texel; the label atlas is 0.24 mm per texel.
- The cover is not animated and the cavity contents are simple.
- Not checked: Windows player build, performance, placement and lighting in the ship, and the import on the
  repository writer's branch. No gameplay is attached.

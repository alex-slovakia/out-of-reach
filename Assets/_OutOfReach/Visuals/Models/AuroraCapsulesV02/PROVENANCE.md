# Provenance: AURORA capsules V02

- **Geometry, UVs, LODs:** original, generated by `Source~/build_capsules.py` in Blender 3.6.1 (`--background`).
  No imported model, kit or scan. Dimensions and pivots follow the team's own `TransportLaunch.fbx`.
- **Textures:** original, painted procedurally with numpy in `Source~/ac_tex.py` (value noise, analytic tile,
  seam, fastener and soot fields). No photograph and no third-party texture.
- **Lettering:** Barlow Condensed Bold, Copyright 2017 The Barlow Project Authors (https://github.com/jpt/barlow), SIL Open Font License 1.1, already
  shipped with its licence in `Assets/_OutOfReach/UI/Typography/`. Used as flat mesh outlines.
- **Design:** fictional AURORA programme craft. The petal docking ring, tiled back shell and ablative base are
  generic spacecraft practice, not a copy of a specific vehicle. No NASA or other agency insignia, no real
  mission markings.
- **Created:** 15 November 2026, task AURORA-CAPSULES-V02, branch `feature/claude-max-rescue-craft`.

# ReferenceSuitsV04 - rights and provenance

- Body, skeleton weights, male shape, garments (casualsuit02 shirt/trousers geometry, AO-derived folds and normal map),
  shoes02 (WORK), shoes03 (EVA boot shell), short02 hair, high-poly eyes and the young caucasian male skin texture:
  **MakeHuman CC0 assets** (CC0-1.0, released by Data Collection AB, Joel Palmius, Jonas Hauquier;
  https://www.makehumancommunity.org). Exact files and sha256 in `MakeHumanCC0-sources.json`.
- The rig is the project's own unchanged FitV02 rig (MakeHuman default skeleton, 164 bones).
- Original work for OUT OF REACH (no third-party licence): the navy knit / charcoal twill / shoe textures
  (`Source~/make_textures.py`), the EVA ivory fabric, hard-goods and display textures (`Source~/make_textures_eva.py`),
  the WORK cargo details (side zip pockets, zips, knee seams, back pockets, rib cuffs and hem), the EVA suit build
  (inflation, gloves, boots, straps, rings, ribs, pockets, chest pack, PLSS, helmet), the male-shape anchoring, the split
  and the scripts.
- No other downloads, scans or AI images. The reference boards only guided proportions and colours.

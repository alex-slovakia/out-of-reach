# ReferenceSuitsV04 - rights and provenance

- Body, skeleton weights, male shape, garments (casualsuit02 shirt/trousers geometry and normal map), shoes02, short02 hair,
  high-poly eyes and the young caucasian male skin texture: **MakeHuman CC0 assets** (CC0-1.0, released by Data Collection AB,
  Joel Palmius, Jonas Hauquier; https://www.makehumancommunity.org). Exact files and sha256 in `MakeHumanCC0-sources.json`.
- The rig is the project's own unchanged FitV02 rig (MakeHuman default skeleton, 164 bones).
- Original work for OUT OF REACH (no third-party licence): the navy knit / charcoal twill / shoe textures (generated by
  `Source~/make_textures.py`), the cargo details (side zip pockets, zips, knee seams, back pockets, rib cuffs and hem), the
  male-shape anchoring, the split and the scripts.
- No other downloads, scans or AI images. The reference board only guided proportions and colours.

## Rig contract (identical to FitV02)
- 164 bones, same names/parents, single root `CharacterRoot`. Bone-name hash (sha256 of names joined by `\n`, first 16 hex) `459a989457d9af1e`.
- Blender: bone heads/tails equal to FitV02 (max 0.7 micrometre), bone matrices max 9e-5. Unity 6000.3.28f1: 164 transforms under `CharacterRoot`, rig local scale (1,1,1), skinned `bindposes` equal to FitV02's by bone name (max element diff 9.8e-5) for every renderer.
- Export: `apply_unit_scale=True`, `FBX_SCALE_UNITS`, `-Z`/`Y`, no leaf bones, no animation clips inside (use FitV02 / FloatNeutralV04 clips on this rig). Importer settings copied from FitV02 minus its clip list.
- FitV02 FBX files store a posed node state; these files store the **bind pose**. Skin binding is identical; drive the pose with your clips as before (Animator root motion off).
- At most 4 influences per vertex, weights sum to 1. All parts are skinned (rigid parts 100 % to `spine02` or `head`); no bone-parented objects, `preserveHierarchy` not needed.
- The head uses MakeHuman's own default weights (head, neck, jaw, eye and face bones of the same skeleton). The FitV02 skeleton is the unmodified MakeHuman default skeleton (similarity fit over all 163 bones: scale 0.10651, residual 0), so the head sits on it without deformation.

## Known limits (honest)
- Shoulders are narrower than the reference man's: the FitV02 base body is the MakeHuman neutral body and was not re-proportioned (rig and body proportions stay identical by contract).
- The face is the MakeHuman neutral base face (smooth, no skin texture, no stubble); hair is a short geometric cut.
- Detail is geometric with solid colours (+ weave normal on cloth): no baked wear, dirt or decals. No NASA marks; the display shows a fictional AURORA-7 UI only.
- Verified: Blender rig compare and Unity batch import of both FBX in an isolated project (no URP there: names and bindings verified, not the URP look). Not verified: in-game URP lighting, clips on these meshes, player/collision fit.

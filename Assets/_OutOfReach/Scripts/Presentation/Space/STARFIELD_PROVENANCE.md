# Distant star background

Original procedural artwork generated by DistantSpaceAuthoring.cs, deterministic seed 74031.
No downloaded images, game assets, brands or external dependencies. Six 1024-pixel faces.
This is an illustrative sky, not a NASA star catalog or a measured spacecraft position.

Use DistantStars.mat as the scene skybox and set the gameplay camera to Skybox clear flags.
The sky depends on camera orientation, not the player's position inside the cabin. Do not
put moving star particles close to the window. Debris belongs to the authored accident.

# Recovery visual presentation provenance

Original team presentation code and planet mesh/shader for OUT OF REACH. Mars uses the explicitly credited historical NASA/JPL color map described below. No generated reference image is embedded.

The arrival shot frames the actual existing AURORA-7 exterior in the scene. Its meshes/materials remain unchanged; their ownership and font credits remain in `Visuals/Models/Aurora7/LICENSES.md`.

The recovery courier is a scene-instance subset of the reviewed corrected `Visuals/Models/MarinePrologue/Transport/Models/TransportLaunch.fbx`: exactly CapsuleShell, CapsuleInterior and Hatch with their existing remapped TL materials. Booster, launch deck, mast, arms and site lights are omitted from the instance. Source geometry, UVs, material assets, metadata and importer axes are not edited. Existing original-team provenance/Inter font credit remains in the Transport PROVENANCE.md.

Mars uses an original mathematical sphere and the NASA Mars Image Texture: Viking images processed by USGS, credit **NASA/Jet Propulsion Laboratory & Caltech**. The unchanged 1440×720 source, URLs, SHA-256 and reuse guidance are recorded in `Visuals/Celestial/RecoveryMars/SOURCE.json`. It is historical composite surface imagery, not live weather or an elevation model. Lighting and the tableau's relative sizes/distances are authored for framing, not calibrated observations or astronomical scale. No NASA endorsement is implied.

Only a confirmed recovered-outcome cinematic request authorizes the first shot. The later Mars scene is explicitly labelled LATER; travel and crew transfer occur offscreen. The approach and second scene are cinematic ellipses, not simulated orbital flight, docking, propulsion or instantaneous rescue.

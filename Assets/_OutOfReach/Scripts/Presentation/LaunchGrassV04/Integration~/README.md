# LAUNCH-GRASS-V04 hand-off (#11 R07)

Branch `feature/claude-max-grass`, rebased on `codex/nasa-2330-staging` @ `022138d` (Tower V03 installed).
Nothing outside `LaunchGrassV04/**` is edited, except Apoliak's TERRAIN-V03 meadow commit (`2a70bd0`,
cherry-picked first, as asked): it changes `GroundFinishV02` and adds the V03 shell meadow that this
installer switches off.

## Install (required: nothing is in Bootstrap yet)

* **Menu:** open `Bootstrap.unity`, then **Tools → OUT OF REACH → Launch Grass V04 - install into open
  scene (no save)**. Review, then save the scene.
* **Batch (saves Bootstrap):**
  `-executeMethod OutOfReach.Presentation.LaunchGrassV04.Editor.LaunchGrassV04Installer.InstallIntoBootstrap`
* Optional composition hook: `git apply` the patch in this folder (adds
  `ClaudeIntegrationAuthoring.ApplyLaunchGrassV04` and two asmdef references).

The installer is idempotent, adds no collider and fails if the active collider set changes. It creates
`PampasGrassV04.mat`, `LaunchHazeDomeV04.mat` and `LaunchCinematicV04.asset` in `Visuals/LaunchGrassV04/`
(commit them with the scene) and resets both materials to the shader defaults on every run. It adds
`Launch grass V04 - pampas field` under `Launch site - stationary` and
`Launch look V04 - grade, haze and thruster gust` beside the site. Re-run it after any change to the
facility: it clears the footprint of every standing renderer outside the apron.

## Terrain rework (LAUNCH-TERRAIN-V04, separate menu item)

Alex asked for the terrain itself to be reworked. It is a second, independent installer:

* **Menu:** **Tools → OUT OF REACH → Launch Terrain V04 - install into open scene (no save)**.
  Batch: `-executeMethod OutOfReach.Presentation.LaunchGrassV04.Editor.LaunchTerrainV04Installer.InstallIntoBootstrap`
* **Undo:** **Launch Terrain V04 - remove (previous terrain back, no save)**.
* It generates 33 meshes from the scene's own `LT_TerrainInner` into `Visuals/LaunchGrassV04/GeneratedTerrain/`
  (about 10 s; commit them with the scene) and adds `Launch terrain V04 - steppe, dry river and ranges`
  under the site, with the existing ground material. No shader is changed.
* It switches off, without deleting: the `LT_TerrainInner` **renderer** (object, mesh and colliders stay; the
  grass still reads its heights) and the `Launch terrain V03 - far land and mountains` group.
* It adds no collider and rolls back if the active collider set changes.

What changes:

* Within 470 m the ground is the old mesh, triangle for triangle (pad hole, apron, crawlerway, grass field).
* The sector towards -Z out to 1.3 km (crawlerway, assembly building, pole line) keeps the old heights.
* Beyond: a plain that rises gently away from the site, twelve hogback ridges 1.2-3 km out in every
  direction except the kept sector, a dry braided river 0.55-1.4 km to the east (up to 30 m deep),
  foothills, and two ranges up to about 1,800 m. The ranges stand closer in the west and north (3.5-4.5 km)
  and further in the east (5-7 km), where the river plain opens towards the sun.
* 581,222 triangles in total (old near ground 71,056, new 510,166). Before: 105,908 + 210,180.

Checked without Unity: the builder was executed on the real `LT_TerrainInner_Microrelief` mesh under the .NET
runtime that ships with Unity. The seam is exact, every triangle faces up, there are no degenerate triangles or
NaNs, and no old height sample is missing. `Evidence~/terrain-blender-*.jpg` are Blender previews of that
output with approximate colours; they show landforms, not the final look.

**Codex: check natively.** Log line `LAUNCH_TERRAIN_V04_READY` with `oldSamplesMissing=0 maxSeamError=0.0000
collidersChanged=0`. Then: no gap or step at 470 m; the assembly building, pole line and crawlerway far end
sit on the ground as before; nothing of the other sets shows on the horizon towards -X; the departure climb
looks right from above; frame time. If anything is wrong, use the remove menu item.

## What it is

* **Modeled clump** (`PampasClumpV04.fbx`, source `Tools~/PampasClumpV04.blend`, generated by
  `Tools~/make_pampas_models.py`): creased leaf blades in a fountain, culm tubes, and seed heads built from
  26 feathery branch cards around the rachis. LOD0 2,856 triangles (to 11 m), LOD1 364 (to 46 m), then two
  crossed cards plus a top-view cap (14 triangles) to 440 m.
* **Four original textures** from `Tools~/make_pampas_textures.py` (tufts, seed heads, whole clumps, clumps
  from above). No source imagery.
* **Wind:** travelling gust waves (62 m, 4.2 m/s), visible as light and dark bands from above.
* **Thruster gust:** shock front from the pad at ignition (9 s), then a sustained push that fades from
  3.5 s after liftoff. Read-only from `TransportDeparturePresenter`.
* **Launch look:** warm grade Volume (weight driven, 0 outside the departure), and distance fog with a
  height falloff from the camera depth texture (fallback: horizon dome). Nothing closer than 70 m is fogged.
* **Clear areas:** apron ±78 × −68..78 m, crawlerway, plume corridor, plus facility footprints. Tower V03
  stands inside the apron rectangle.
* **Reduced effects** (from the departure's `ReducedMotion`): 55 % density, 65 % draw distance, half gust,
  no grain, lighter bloom.

## Cost

| | |
|---|---|
| Clumps | about 267,000 in 937 chunks (24 m) and about 7,750 cells (8 m) |
| GPU memory | 8.5 MB instance buffer (two copies), four atlases (1024×1024 and smaller, compressed) |
| Generation | 0.6-1.2 s once per enable, on a worker thread in Play mode |
| Draw calls | roughly 220-300 per camera |

Frame time is **not measured for the final state**. Last measurement (scratch URP project, RTX 3070,
1600×900, model in, but still submitting whole 24 m chunks with the heavy mesh): +5.5 to +6.5 ms at eye
level and +3.8 ms from 25 m up, 4.3-7.5 M triangles. Per-cell drawing and the shorter model distances were
added after that to cut the modeled triangles to roughly a quarter; my estimate is +3 to +4 ms. Before the
model (cards and ribbons only) it was +2.8 to +3.7 ms.

If it is too slow natively: lower `detailDistance` / `modelDistance` on the field (11 / 46 m), or
`maxDrawDistance` (440 m).

## Codex: check natively

   `NONE`, `collidersChanged=0`. Note `footprintsCleared`.
2. Both shaders compile in the real project (Forward+). They are unchanged since their last native compile.
3. **Hand-over between levels at 11 m and 46 m** while walking into the field: no holes, no doubled or
   missing clumps. This is the one part that was never rendered (see below).
4. No grass through the pad, Tower V03, crawlerway or plume corridor.
5. Departure: gust at ignition, grade blends in and out, no pop when the site switches off at 17.5 s.
6. Depth fog: horizon gradient, sky band, and how the sea and transparent effects sit against it. If it
   looks wrong, untick `depthFog` on the look object (falls back to the dome).
7. Frame time from the boarding platform and at ground level; reduced effects; pause holds.
8. The depth fog is lighter now (0.16 per km, 500 m falloff) so that the new mid-ground keeps its contrast.

The board is generated concept art based on an actual Unity capture. It is not gameplay proof or a raster background to substitute for the game.

Match: prominent white mm:ss timer centered above the view, steady red at10seconds; compact original pictograms with segmented oxygen/food/battery bars; two physically aligned hands carrying the actual cargo case; readable stow prompt; warm practical light, cool shadows, restrained vignette and subtle bloom. Keep the corridor geometry and playable clearance. The environment dominates the frame. No giant HUD cards, decorative holograms or raw resource numbers in the normal view; exact quantities remain in the systems detail. Cargo mass and launch limits remain visible for decisions.

The concept's exertion bar requires a real stamina authority first. Food stores are not physiological hunger. Oxygen reserve is not cabin oxygen concentration. Runtime labels must preserve those distinctions. The depicted case/hands require real geometry/animation and must be verified in Unity, not claimed from this board.

Staged sequence: UI bars/countdown; graded actual lighting; two-hand cargo grip; countdown beeps/pulse; scene-bound repair animation; separate authored launch sequence. Domain sprint/stamina and post-contact survival are coordinated with Claude.

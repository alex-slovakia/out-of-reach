# LAND-TERRAIN-V01: bounded landscape for the land launch site

Replaces the flat, brown, empty launch ground (playtest AM03) with a bounded, original landscape for a fictional coastal land launch site:

- relief that is exactly flat where the LAND kit and the corridors need it, and gentle coastal relief elsewhere (dunes and swales near the site, rolling ground of 10–20 m at 2–3 km, a low ridge band on the horizon);
- a varied ground material (dry and green grass, scrub, sand, damp soil, burn scars, maintained ground, a blast-sand fan in the plume corridor);
- 470 embedded rocks in 62 clusters;
- a far ring out to 9 km, so the horizon is closed.

No mountains, no water, no logos. Nothing in the LAND kit or in the game scene is changed by this package.

**Status:** art package for root's native check. Blender round trip, footprint boundary check and an isolated Unity import check pass (numbers below). It is not imported into the game scene, and the material is not checked in Unity.

![deck north, deck east, aerial, ground](renders/LT_sheet.png)

The renders are Cycles previews with a stand-in sun and sky and a simple distance haze (1 − e^(−d / 10 km), added in post). They are not runtime results.

## Placement

- Unity metres, y up, local to the prologue origin. **Every object's pivot is the prologue origin.** Put the FBX root there with identity rotation and scale 1.
- **Model importer:** the ship's settings, **with Bake Axis Conversion ON**:
  - `bakeAxisConversion 1`, `useFileUnits 1`, `useFileScale 1`, `globalScale 1`;
  - no cameras or lights, `preserveHierarchy 0`;
  - Normals: Import (the terrain carries analytic normals); Tangents: Calculate Mikktspace (needed by the detail normal map);
  - Index Format Auto (every mesh is under 65,535 vertices, so 16-bit); no colliders are generated by this package.
  - **With Bake Axis Conversion off, everything lands at (−x, y, −z).**
- **Remove or disable root's flat ground plane at y −36.6.** The terrain lies exactly on y −36.6 in the footprint apron and both corridors, so the two would z-fight.
- **Camera far plane:** the far ring ends at 9 km. Use a far clip plane of at least 10 km, or fog that ends the view before 9 km.
- **LAND-SURROUNDINGS v01** stands on the constant datum and would float or sink on the new relief. Use its v02, which is re-seated on this height field (second package in this folder).

## Terrain interface (kept flat)

- **Footprint hole:** exactly the rectangle x [−72, 72], z [−62, 72]. The inner boundary vertices are exactly the LAND kit's boundary vertices, all at y −36.6:
  - x = ±72 at z −62, −50, 60 and 72;
  - z = −62 at x ±72 and ±60;
  - z = +72 at x ±72, ±60, ±4 and ±3 (trench walls and floor).
  - There is no other vertex on the footprint edge, so there is no T-junction against the foundation.
- **Flat zones** (h = 0): the footprint, the crawlerway and facility corridor x −55..95, z −1,020..−50, and the plume and outlet corridor x ±35, z 60..320.
  - h stays exactly 0 within 6 m of every edge of these zones, then blends in with a smootherstep over 64 m (full relief from 70 m).
  - The crawlerway lanes (tops 0.15 m above the datum, bottoms 0.2 m below) and the facility therefore sit on flat ground.
- **Drainage:** two 0.7 m ditches follow LAND-SURROUNDINGS' drainage lines (x −100, z −40..250 and z 150, x 48..190). They also stay at 0 within 6 m of the zones.
- **Rocks** keep 10 m plus their size clear of every zone, 6 m clear of the fence line, 9 m clear of the pole line and 7 m clear of the ditches.

## Height field

The ground is y = −36.6 + h(x, z), with h = M(d) · R(x, z). It is defined in `source/lt_height.py` (pure NumPy, fixed integer hash), and its full formula is in `lt_manifest.json` under `height_field`. Other kits can seat objects with `lt_height.ground_y(x, z)` or `lt_height.seat_y(x, z, radius)`.

- **d:** smooth distance to the union of the three flat zones (smooth-min, k 24 m).
- **M:** `smootherstep((d − 6) / 64)`.
- **R**, the coastal relief:

| Term | What it does | Size |
|---|---|---|
| dunes | Shore-parallel ridges along z, about 60 m apart. Higher on the coast side (+x); faded out by 3 km, where the mesh can no longer resolve them. | up to about 4 m |
| swales | Shallow depressions. | down to −1.3 m |
| swells | Mid-scale undulation. | ±1 m near, ±4 m from 1.3 km |
| rolling ground | Amplitude grows with distance. | about 1.5 m near, 10–20 m at 2–3 km |
| far swells | Broad swells from 2.6 km. | about ±8 m |
| rim | A low ridge band at 6.6–9 km that closes the horizon seen from the crew deck. | 16–32 m |

Inside the security fence (x ±200, z −200..330), dunes and swales are graded to 75 %.

Sampled from h (200,000 random points per band, outside the blend; slopes from the vertex-normal formula below):

| Distance from origin | h, 1st–99th percentile | Steepest slope |
|---|---|---|
| 80–300 m | −2.4..3.1 m | 11° |
| 300–800 m | −2.1..5.7 m | 10° |
| 1.5–3 km | −14..21 m | 13° |
| 6–9 km | −11..42 m (max 53 m) | 8° |

Vertex normals come from the gradient of h, sampled over half the local mesh spacing. The inner/far seam, which shares its vertices, therefore shades without a line.

## Materials (Unity URP Lit, no custom shader)

UV0 of both terrain meshes is the macro map's planar mapping:
- u = U(x), v = U(z), with U(c) = 0.5 + c / 6,400 for |c| ≤ 3,000 m (3.125 m per texel);
- from 3,000 to 9,000 m the far ring continues into the map's outer 64 px band.

The image top is +z and the right edge is +x.

**`LT_Terrain`** (`LT_TerrainInner`):
- Base Map `LT_Terrain_Macro`, tiling 1 1, colour white.
- Metallic 0, Smoothness 0.1, no base Normal Map.
- **Detail Inputs:**
  - Mask: none;
  - Base Map `LT_TerrainDetail_Albedo`, scale 1;
  - Normal Map `LT_TerrainDetail_Normal`, scale 1;
  - **Tiling 1280 × 1280**, Offset 0 (one 5 m tile).

**`LT_TerrainFar`** (`LT_TerrainFar`):
- the same `LT_Terrain_Macro` asset on UV0, tiling 1 1;
- Smoothness 0.08, no normal map, no Detail Inputs.

**`LT_Rock`** (`LT_Rocks`):
- LAND's `LS_Concrete` maps (BaseColor, Normal, MODS);
- Base Color #8C8579, tiling 0.5. The rock UVs are world-scale (1 UV = 1 m), so one tile is 2 m.

**Fallback (tiled BaseMap only):**
- `LT_Terrain`: Base Map `LT_TerrainTile_BaseColor`, Tiling 400 × 400 (one 16 m tile), Normal Map `LT_TerrainDetail_Normal` scale 0.6, no Detail Inputs.
- `LT_TerrainFar`: Base Color #7C7F57, no map.
- `LT_Rock`: Base Color #8C8579, no map.

**Texture importer:**

| Map | Size | Settings |
|---|---|---|
| `LT_Terrain_Macro` | 2048² | Default, sRGB, **Wrap Clamp**, mipmaps, Aniso 8 |
| `LT_TerrainDetail_Albedo` | 1024² | Default, **sRGB on**, Repeat. Neutral: sRGB 188 = linear 0.5, so URP's "Detail Albedo ×2" keeps the macro colour on average. |
| `LT_TerrainDetail_Normal` | 1024² | Normal map (OpenGL +Y), Repeat |
| `LT_TerrainTile_BaseColor` | 1024² | Default, sRGB, Repeat; fallback only |

The macro map is coloured from each texel's world position and the same height field:
- dry and greener grass;
- scrub communities with thickets and openings;
- pale dune grass and sand on the coast-side crests, blowouts and steeper faces;
- damp soil in swales and ditches;
- old burn-scar blocks;
- mowed ground inside the fence and along the corridors;
- a hardstand at the facility;
- blast sand with a soot fan from the trench outlet;
- a gravel strip at the foundation's slope foot;
- faint sandy service tracks;
- darker wooded ground towards the horizon.

All maps are procedural (`source/lt_textures.py`).

## Rebuild

Needs Blender 5.3, and Python 3 with NumPy and Pillow. `<land>` is `docs/art-direction/land-launch-site-v01`.

```
python source/lt_textures.py <out>/textures
blender -b --factory-startup --python source/build_terrain.py -- --out <out> --textures <out>/textures --land-textures <dir with LS_, A7_Paint_, A7_Brushed_ maps and ls_markings.json> --land-src <land>/source [--render --samples 32]
blender -b --factory-startup --python source/roundtrip_habitat.py -- --out <out> --report lt_build_report.json
blender -b --factory-startup --python source/check_terrain.py -- --out <out> --land-fbx <land>/LS_LandLaunchSite.fbx
python source/make_sheet.py <out>/renders LT
python source/make_manifest.py <package dir>
```

- Fixed seeds and a fixed hash, so every run gives the same geometry and maps.
- The .blend's preview context (`PV_*`: the LAND kit, a vehicle and mast proxy, sky, sun, cameras) is not exported.
- `unity_check/LTCheck.cs` goes into an Editor folder. Run it with `-executeMethod LTCheck.Run -ltFbx Assets/LT/LT_LandTerrain.fbx -ltOut lt_check.tsv`, then `python unity_check/lt_compare.py lt_check.tsv lt_build_report.json`.

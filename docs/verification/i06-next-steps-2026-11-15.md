## Playtest refinements

- Loading ramp. Natural alternating arm movement, a calmer leg swing and a steady eye line. Test steep look-down while walking. For the 25 kg cargo case, test ascent and turning with both hands on their actual grip markers and clearance at the knees.
- Belt. Keep the first-person eye movement connected to the motion of sitting down. The subsequent departure sequence, docking, ship lighting and departure music received positive playtest feedback. The approved [launch board](../art-direction/launch-reference-v01/README.md) guides the detailed engine, pad and steam work.
- Loading music. Approved direction is a low bass pulse with soft metal strikes that build near the end of the clock. Keep the departure score. Treat cockpit music as one local console speaker. Tune distance falloff, door occlusion and modest interior reflections, with dialogue priority. The positions and room attenuation are game tuning. Pouch handling, storage opening and berth fastening animations remain. Use ISS retort/rehydration pouches, Veggie plant pillows and wall-mounted sleeping-bag restraints as reference.

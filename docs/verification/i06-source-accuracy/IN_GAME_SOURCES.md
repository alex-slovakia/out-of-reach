# Suggested optional Sources view copy

**Reference data.** Earth-Mars range comes from NASA/JPL Horizons: 32 daily geometric samples, 14 November-15 December 2026 at 08:00 UTC, kilometres, ICRF. Range divided by light speed supplies an educational signal-delay approximation. AURORA-7's Mars-vicinity position is fictional; this is not spacecraft telemetry or a calculated rescue route. [Horizons](https://ssd-api.jpl.nasa.gov/doc/horizons.html)

**Star background.** Star directions come from NASA GMAT's star graphics data, distributed under Apache-2.0. Approximate source magnitudes are rendered with artistic brightness and point spread. Stellar observation epoch is unspecified; no precision navigation or Hipparcos attribution is claimed. [GMAT](https://github.com/nasa/GMAT)

**Survival model.** NASA life-support references inform crew-day resource quantities. The Veggie reference informs the cultivation chapter; the starting plants, costs, harvest credit and failure rules are game assumptions. Rest advances the same resource/calendar model. The nearby recovery craft and its schedule are fictional. [NASA Veggie](https://science.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/veggie_fact_sheet_508.pdf), [NASA sleep opportunity](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/6-0-medical-operations/)

**Dialogue.** Original dialogue and sound design: OUT OF REACH. Synthetic dialogue: Google Gemini TTS, Orus. These are prerecorded fictional performances, not NASA recordings. Generated voices are not labeled CC0. Production provenance and the recorded permitted-use basis are included with the project. [Recorded Gemini terms reference](https://aistudio.google.com/docs/terms)
